Material Symbols glyphs as SVG, under the Apache Licence 2.0.

Extracted from the bundled variable font (`assets/fonts/material-symbols-outlined`)
at `FILL 0` and `FILL 1`, `wght 400`, `GRAD 0`, `opsz 24`, so the two files are
the same glyph at the two ends of one axis rather than two drawings.

They exist to show the icon-registry path, where FILL is not available: a
registered icon is one SVG, so a filled counterpart is a second registered icon.
See `_components/buttons.md`.
